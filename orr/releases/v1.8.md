# ORR 1.8: fix what 1.7 measured

*Released 2026-10-01. Default in-season model: `--model 1.8`. Models 1.7 through 1.0 remain reproducible. The frozen 2026-27 preseason file is unchanged.*

## Why

1.7's rest-of-season intervals were better but still too narrow, at 73% coverage, and its best setting sat at the edge of the grid. Goalies had no rest-of-season file. The website's team-statistics table ignored the in-season view. No forecast said what had changed since the last one.

## What is new

| Feature | Switch | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Rest-of-season intervals, second pass.** A wider rate-variance grid plus an injury-spell term: each player's historical chance of a long absence removes a block of his remaining games (`backtest/ros_interval2_bt.py`). | `ros_interval` | **On** (adopted: v = 6, spells) |
| **Goalie rest-of-season file** `goalies_ros_<date>.csv`. Season-to-date starts, shots against and save %. Rest-of-season starts are binomial on the updated start share; season save % comes from the talent posterior. 80% intervals by simulation. | `goalie_ros` | On |
| **Team statistics follow the View selector.** In the in-season view, GF/GA come from the daily run, and SF/SA from box scores so far plus the rest-of-season player and goalie lines. | always | On |
| **Forecast diff.** Each game's change in P(home win) since its previous forecast (an earlier run that day, else the preseason file), with what changed: starters, lineups or ratings. Shown as "Δ since last" on the Today table. | `forecast_diff` | On |

## Evidence: rest-of-season intervals

- **Setup.** As in 1.7 (checkpoints at 25%, 50% and 75%; 80% interval score; tuning on 2012 and 2014-2017).
- **Spell probability.** A smoothed share of the player's last three seasons with 15+ games missed. A spell occurs in the remaining games with that probability, scaled by the share of the season left, and removes a uniform 0-60% of his remaining games.

- **Decision.** Adopted under the pre-declared rule.
- **Coverage.** Test coverage is now at the nominal 80%.

## Evidence: other features

No new tuning. `test_live_1_8` checks:
- the forecast diff against an earlier run and against the preseason file, with the reasons it reports;
- that each team's goalie rest-of-season starts sum to most of its 84 games (the rest are call-ups);
- that the save-% intervals bracket their means.

## Limits

- **First live use.** The forecast diff and goalie file take effect from the first 1.8 daily run. The 2026-10-01 forecast was made by 1.7, and its games had started when 1.8 shipped, so it was not regenerated.
- **Ad hoc spell model.** The spell term's block size (uniform 0-60%) is a fixed assumption, not tuned.
