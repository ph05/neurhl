# Figure notes

All figures are regenerated by `paper/figures/make_figures.py` from files committed
in the repository (see the script's docstring for the command). Each figure exists
as a vector PDF for LaTeX and a 200-dpi PNG for the web. Full-width figures are
6.3 in wide (`\includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{...}`); `fig_reliability` is
3.1 in wide (half width).

Conventions used in every figure:

- Seasons are named by the year they end (2018 = 2017-18). All games are regular season.
- Colours match `paper/main.tex`: NeurHL-G blue (`gcol`, #0072B2), NeurHL-H orange
  (`hcol`, #D55E00), Elo grey (`elocol`, #7F7F7F). The NeurHL 1.0 player-game layer
  is green (#009E73) and house expected goals purple (#CC79A7); observed outcomes
  are black.
- Log loss is in nats per game (natural logarithm, probabilities clipped at 1e-9).
  Log-loss differences are drawn in units of 10^-3 nats per game; negative values
  favour the first-named model.
- Unless a caption says otherwise, 95% intervals are the mean plus or minus 1.96
  paired standard errors, treating games as independent.
- The script prints 67 checks against the numbers quoted in the repository's records
  (JSON outputs, the NeurHL-G search ledger, PLAN_NeurHL4.md, EVIDENCE.md); all 67
  reproduce.

## Figure: `fig_windows` (.pdf, .png)

**Description.** Season roles in the two evaluation protocols. Each cell is one
regular season. *NeurHL 1.0* (top): 2008 training only; 2009-2011 development
(architecture and calibration choices); 2012-2017 tune (spent by v1, development
only thereafter; 6,150 scored games); 2018-2026 confirmation, spent once per layer
(NeurHL-H's game-level confirmation scored 10,184 games in eight seasons); 2027,
the 2026-27 season, live. *NeurHL-G* (bottom): 2008-2011 burn-in (player state and
training only); G_ITER, 2012 and 2014-2018, for iteration and ablations (7,421
games); G_GATE, 2019, 2020 and 2022-2024, for the gates and the S-STOP pre-gate
(6,289 games); SEALED, 2025-2026, the one-shot test (2,624 games), not spent; 2027
live (1,344 scheduled games). Hatched cells, 2013 (48-game lockout season) and 2021
(56-game COVID season), are trained on but never scored. Shading darkens with the
protection of a window: training only, development, tuning or gating, one-shot
test, live. Every NeurHL-G window had already been used at game level by NeurHL
1.0, so its seal is procedural rather than unseen.

- What is plotted: season-to-window assignment only; no estimates.
- Window: 2008-2027.
- Source: `neurhl/windows.py` (TRAIN_FROM, DEV, TUNE, CONFIRM, PROJECT, NO_SCORE,
  SEASON_NOTES, G_BURN_IN, G_ITER, G_GATE, SEALED, G_LIVE). Game counts in the
  caption: `neurhl/output/hier_result.json` (6,150), `neurhl/output/hier_restatement.json`
  (10,184), `neurhl/output/preds/g_iter_ladder_games.csv` (7,421),
  `neurhl/output/preds/g_gate_games.csv` (6,289), PLAN_NeurHL4.md section W (2,624; 1,344).

## Figure: `fig_c1_seasons` (.pdf, .png)

**Description.** The one-shot confirmation of NeurHL-H against Elo (PLAN_NeurHL
amendment A5.2). Points: mean paired per-game log-loss difference, NeurHL-H minus
Elo, in each regular season 2018-2026, with 95% confidence intervals; negative
values favour NeurHL-H. Numbers under the season labels are games per season.
Filled points are the eight seasons of the primary test (2018-2020, 2022-2026); 2021
(hollow, grey) is excluded from the primary test by the never-score rule and is
shown for completeness. The shaded band and dashed line mark the pooled primary
estimate, repeated as the diamond at right: -0.00478 (95% CI -0.00650 to -0.00306;
n = 10,184 games; log loss 0.66495 against 0.66973). The hollow diamond pools all
nine seasons including 2021: -0.00459 (95% CI -0.00622 to -0.00295; n = 11,052).
NeurHL-H is better in 8 of the 8 primary seasons (season-clustered t test
p = 0.0007; per-game z test p = 4.9e-8).

- What is plotted: per-season and pooled mean of nll(p_neurhl_h, y) - nll(p_elo, y),
  scaled by 10^3, with mean +/- 1.96 SE (SE = SD / sqrt(n) within the season or pool).
- n: 1,271 (2018), 1,271 (2019), 1,082 (2020), 868 (2021, excluded), 1,312 in each
  of 2022-2026; pooled 10,184 (primary) and 11,052 (with 2021).
- Window: confirmation, regular seasons 2018-2026; spent once.
- Source: `neurhl/output/hier_restatement.json` (blocks `primary` and
  `including_2021`, with their `per_season` entries). Every per-season estimate and
  interval is recomputed from `neurhl/output/preds/hier_restatement_games.csv` and
  agrees with the JSON to within 1e-6.

## Figure: `fig_reliability` (.pdf, .png; half width)

**Description.** Reliability of the NeurHL-H (orange) and Elo (grey) home-win
probabilities on the 10,184 games of the primary confirmation set (regular seasons
2018-2020 and 2022-2026). Top: for each model, games are sorted by predicted
home-win probability and split into ten equal-count bins of 1,018-1,019 games; each
point is a bin's mean predicted probability against its observed home-win frequency,
with a 95% Wilson interval. The solid diagonal is perfect calibration; the dashed
line is the observed home-win rate in these games (0.540). Bottom: histograms of the
predicted probabilities (bin width 0.02). NeurHL-H spreads its forecasts more widely
than Elo while staying on the diagonal, in line with the Murphy decomposition
recorded for this test: reliability 0.00021 against 0.00025, resolution 0.0122
against 0.0098.

- What is plotted: equal-count (decile) bins of each model's own predictions; bin
  mean of p against bin mean of y.
- n: 10,184 games (ten bins of 1,018-1,019 per model).
- Window: confirmation, primary set (2021 excluded).
- Source: `neurhl/output/preds/hier_restatement_games.csv` (columns p_neurhl_h,
  p_elo, y, season). Murphy terms: `neurhl/output/hier_restatement.json`,
  `primary.murphy` (20 fixed-width bins).

## Figure: `fig_g_gate_seasons` (.pdf, .png)

**Description.** NeurHL-G on its gate window, G_GATE (regular seasons 2019, 2020
and 2022-2024; 2021 is never scored). Left: mean paired per-game log-loss difference
by season for NeurHL-G's final probability (the stack over Elo, NeurHL-G and
NeurHL-H) minus Elo (filled circles) and minus NeurHL-H (open squares), with 95%
confidence intervals; numbers under the season labels are games. Right: the same
differences pooled over 6,289 games: NeurHL-G minus Elo -0.00559 (SE 0.00131; 95%
CI -0.00817 to -0.00302) and NeurHL-G minus NeurHL-H -0.00049 (SE 0.00060; 95% CI
-0.00167 to +0.00069); the pooled log losses are 0.66006 (NeurHL-G), 0.66566 (Elo)
and 0.66056 (NeurHL-H). Dotted segments are the preregistered S-STOP thresholds,
-0.0045 against Elo and -0.0010 against NeurHL-H, each also requiring the same
direction in at least 4 of the 5 seasons. The Elo condition holds (better in 5 of 5
seasons); the NeurHL-H condition fails (3 of 5), so the sealed test was not spent.

- What is plotted: per-season and pooled means of nll(p_stack, y) - nll(p_elo, y)
  and nll(p_stack, y) - nll(p_h, y), scaled by 10^3, with mean +/- 1.96 SE.
- n: 1,271 (2019), 1,082 (2020), 1,312 in each of 2022-2024; pooled 6,289.
- Window: G_GATE, one run of candidate g1 (ledger row g1:full:gate).
- Source: `neurhl/output/preds/g_gate_games.csv` (p_stack, p_elo, p_h, y,
  season_end). Pooled means, SEs and the per-season Elo differences match
  `neurhl/output/g_gates.json` (block S_STOP) to within 1e-6. Thresholds are read
  from `neurhl/eval/gate_g.py` and equal PLAN_NeurHL4.md section G.

## Figure: `fig_g_ladder` (.pdf, .png)

**Description.** The NeurHL-G iteration ladder on G_ITER (regular seasons 2012
and 2014-2018; 7,421 games). Each point is the pooled log loss of a full run's
final probability, a walk-forward logistic stack over Elo and the network (and,
for R11, NeurHL-H), with the network's outputs averaged over 3 seeds. R6: the network with outcome fine-tuning
(the parent); R6e: R6 with an Elo anchor on the scoring rates; R6L: R6 with
lineup-versus-usual team-rate multipliers; R6c: R6 with NeurHL-H lineup projections
as team-encoder inputs; R13: R6c with direct terms on the NeurHL-H projections;
R9: R6c with self-attention across both rosters; R11: R6 with NeurHL-H added to the
stack. Open circles were rejected under the declared adoption rule (keep a component
only if it cuts G_ITER log loss by at least 0.0003 without losing in 4 or more of the
6 seasons, or meets the declared box-score margins); the filled diamond, R11, was
carried forward as candidate g1 (run with 5 seeds at the gate). Horizontal lines:
Elo (grey, 0.67528) and NeurHL-H alone (orange, 0.67244) on the same games. The
paired standard error of each rung's difference from NeurHL-H is 0.0007-0.0009,
larger than most gaps between rungs. Not shown: R4, the non-neural engine, which ran
only as a quick run on 2016 and 2018 (2,501 games; 0.67581).

- What is plotted: mean nll over the 7,421 games for columns p_r6, p_r6e, p_r6L,
  p_r6c, p_r13, p_r9, p_r11 (points) and p_elo, p_h (lines). No intervals are drawn.
- Values: R6 0.67301, R6e 0.67330, R6L 0.67299, R6c 0.67344, R13 0.67344,
  R9 0.67366, R11 0.67258.
- Window: G_ITER.
- Source: `neurhl/output/preds/g_iter_ladder_games.csv`. Every value matches the
  latest full-run row of each rung in `neurhl/configs/search_ledger_g.csv` (R6 and
  R11 at fb6404c, R6e at 87eee43, R6L at 1049507, R6c, R13 and R9 at 97ad318) to
  within 1e-7, and the CANDIDATE table of PLAN_NeurHL4.md to five decimals.
- Provenance note: R6, R11, R6c, R13 and R9 were run on the master tensor rebuilt on
  official PP opportunities (commit 97ad318). R6e (87eee43) and R6L (1049507) were
  run before that rebuild, when their parent was the earlier R6 run at ae688bf
  (0.67299; the earlier R11 run there scored 0.67297). Against that parent R6e is
  +0.00031 and R6L -0.000004, so neither decision changes, but the plotted R6 (0.67301)
  is not the run those two rungs were compared with. The ledger's config hash for
  R6e (c1b326eed41c) also differs from the committed r6e.json (28f5b838d820).

## Figure: `fig_player_heads` (.pdf, .png)

**Description.** Player-level predictions, relative change in loss with 95%
confidence intervals; negative values are improvements. (a) The NeurHL 1.0
player-game layer against each skater's own recent average (an exponentially
weighted average; for goals and assists shrunk towards the previous season's league
rate) on its one-shot confirmation, regular seasons 2018-2020: 130,092 skater-games
of 1,152 skaters in 3,624 games. Ice-time share MAE -4.1% (95% CI -4.5 to -3.8),
shots-on-goal Poisson deviance -10.2% (-11.6 to -8.7), P(goal) log loss -2.8% (-3.4
to -2.1), P(assist) log loss -2.6% (-2.9 to -2.2). (b) NeurHL-G's player heads
against that player-game layer on G_GATE (2019, 2020, 2022-2024): 225,752
skater-games in 6,289 games. Ice-time share -0.59% (-0.79 to -0.40), shots on goal
-0.45% (-0.55 to -0.36), P(goal) -0.45% (-0.64 to -0.26), P(assist) -0.36% (-0.51
to -0.21). The dotted line is the PG gate's non-inferiority margin (+1%, applied to
the upper bound). Relative change is the mean paired difference divided by the mean
baseline loss; standard errors are two-way clustered by skater and game (Cameron,
Gelbach and Miller, 2011). The two panels have different scales.

- What is plotted: (a) 100 x relative_to_baseline, interval +/- 1.96 x |relative / z_twoway|;
  (b) 100 x rel_diff, interval from 2 x rel_diff - rel_upper95 to rel_upper95
  (the gate script's interval is symmetric, mean +/- 1.96 two-way SE).
- Loss definitions: (a) true Poisson deviance for shots
  (`eval/backtest_player_game.py`); (b) the Poisson loss mu - y log(mu) used by
  `eval/gate_g.py`, which differs from the deviance by a term in y only, so the
  paired difference is the same up to a factor of 2 but the denominator of the
  relative change is not. The tick label therefore reads "Poisson loss".
- n: (a) 130,092 skater-games; (b) 225,752 skater-games.
- Windows: (a) player confirmation 2018-2020, spent once; (b) G_GATE.
- Sources: (a) `neurhl/configs/player_game_twoway_2018_2020.json` (checked against
  `neurhl/configs/player_game_confirm_2018_2020.json`); (b) `neurhl/output/g_gates.json`,
  block PG.

## Figure: `fig_xg_drift` (.pdf, .png)

**Description.** League goals and house expected goals (xG) per team-game by
regular season, 2010-2026. (a) Mean goals per team-game (black) and mean house xG
per team-game (purple), with 95% confidence bands from game-level standard errors.
Both count all strengths, including empty-net play and overtime, and exclude
shootouts. (b) The ratio of goals to xG, with a 95% delta-method band; the line
marks 1. Goals per team-game held near 3.0 in 2024-2026 (3.08, 3.01, 3.08) while xG
rose (2.89, 3.03, 3.32), so the ratio fell from 1.067 in 2024 to 0.927 in 2026. The
house xG is walk-forward: each season's shots are scored by a model fitted on
earlier seasons, with an in-season calibrator that uses only games already played
(`neurhl/train/train_xg.py`). 2013 (720 games) and 2021 (868 games) were shortened seasons; the
others have 1,229-1,312 games.

- What is plotted: season means of gf_all and xgf_all over team-games (= game totals
  / 2) and their ratio (mean goals / mean xG).
- n: team-games per season = 2 x games: 1,440 in 2013, 1,736 in 2021 and
  2,458-2,624 otherwise (column n_team_games of the CSV).
- Window: 2010-2026, every regular season.
- Source: per-team-game tables `neurhl/data/tensors/tgx_{season}.parquet` (columns
  gf_all, xgf_all; built by `neurhl/data/build_xg_games.py`; gitignored), aggregated
  by the script to the committed `paper/figures/data/xg_drift.csv`, which also holds
  the empty-net-excluded variants (goals_no_en_pg, xg_no_en_pg).
- Definition check: the plotted definition (all strengths, including empty-net and
  overtime goals, from gf_all and xgf_all) reproduces the PLAN_NeurHL4 A1 numbers
  exactly. Excluding empty-net play gives 2.84, 2.73, 2.81 goals and 2.65, 2.75, 3.03
  xG in 2024-2026 (CSV columns goals_no_en_pg, xg_no_en_pg), and counting only
  regulation goals gives 3.01 in 2024 (from the event stream,
  neurhl/data/tensors/stream_2024.parquet, periods 1-3); neither matches.

## Figure: `fig_outcome_calib` (.pdf, .png)

**Description.** Outcome calibration of NeurHL-G on G_GATE (regular seasons 2019,
2020 and 2022-2024; 6,289 games). (a) Reliability of the final (stacked) home-win
probability: ten equal-count bins of 628-629 games, bin mean prediction against
observed home-win frequency, with 95% Wilson intervals; solid diagonal, perfect
calibration; dashed line, the observed home-win rate (0.534). The logistic
calibration slope (outcome regressed on the logit of the prediction, with intercept)
is 0.968 (95% CI 0.867-1.069); gate C required a slope in [0.9, 1.1]. (b) Share of
games tied after regulation, and so decided in overtime or a shootout, by season:
observed share with 95% Wilson interval (black) and the engine's mean predicted
probability of a regulation tie (blue). Pooled: 22.4% predicted against 22.0%
observed (95% CI 21.0-23.0%), inside the gate's one-point tolerance. Gate C as
declared nevertheless fails, on team-SOG interval coverage (PLAN_NeurHL4 A2), which
this figure does not show.

- What is plotted: (a) decile bins of p_stack against y; (b) per season, mean of
  p_tie against the share of games with outcome4 in {2, 3} (2 = home win, 3 = away
  win, both after regulation; 0 and 1 are regulation wins).
- Per season, predicted vs observed (%): 2019 23.2 vs 21.3; 2020 23.2 vs 23.1;
  2022 22.2 vs 22.0; 2023 21.4 vs 23.0; 2024 22.1 vs 20.7.
- n: 6,289 games (1,271; 1,082; 1,312; 1,312; 1,312).
- Window: G_GATE.
- Source: `neurhl/output/preds/g_gate_games.csv` (p_stack, y, p_tie, outcome4,
  season_end). Slope and OT shares match `neurhl/output/g_gates.json`, block C
  (slope to 1e-6 with a plain Newton fit in place of scikit-learn's C = 1e6).

goal-level calibration of PLAN_NeurHL4 A1.

- What is plotted: decile bins of the pooled predictions sogf_h/sogf_a,
  xgf_h/xgf_a, goals_h/goals_a against y_sogf_h/y_sogf_a, y_xgf_all_h/y_xgf_all_a,
  y_gf_reg_h/y_gf_reg_a (1,253-1,258 team-games per bin).
- n: 12,578 team-games for SOG and xG; 12,536 for regulation goals.
- Window: G_GATE.
- Source: `neurhl/output/preds/g_gate_games.csv`.
