Methodology

Name
ORR stands for Odds, Ratings & Rosters, its three layers: the betting market's season lines, team ratings from a game model, and roster-based player projections.
Information cutoff
The 2026-27 forecast uses only information dated before 2026-09-29 17:00 ET, the first puck drop. Every repository input is read from the last commit before that time, and each input file's SHA-256 is recorded. The forecast was built on 2026-09-30 and 2026-10-01, after the season began, so it is scored only on games played after its publication.
Data
MoneyPuck season summaries and game results from 2005-06 to 2025-26; per-game box scores from 2010-11 to 2023-24 (starting goalies, ice time, shots); rosters, roster changes and injury reports up to 2026-09-29; the sportsbook season points lines and Stanley Cup odds recorded on 2026-08-17; historical preseason points lines for 2018-19 to 2025-26; and 46 researched injury, suspension and holdout timelines with dated sources.
Standings target
Each team's expected points are 75% the de-vigged sportsbook line and 25% a regressed team-history view. The weights were chosen leave-one-season-out from a fixed list of combinations; they are non-negative and sum to one, so the forecast is never more spread out than its inputs. Moves after 2026-08-17, which the line had not seen, are priced with the player model by undoing each one: trades, signings, contract terminations, camp injuries, suspensions and the Hellebuyck holdout (a 10% chance he plays for Winnipeg).
Game model
Points targets are converted into offence and defence ratings on the actual schedule. Every game is scored with one model: a Conway-Maxwell-Poisson goal distribution, a late-game pulled-goalie layer that preserves the mean, era-specific overtime and shootout models, rest and travel effects, and starting-goalie effects. Preseason game probabilities average over 400 joint draws of both teams' ratings.
Player projections
Per-60 rates for goals, assists, shots and other events are regressed toward position averages by exposure, adjusted for age, and translated from other leagues for rookies, with draft position as a prior. A depth-chart model decides who dresses, ice time is conserved within each team, and player goals are reconciled to team goals so that player, team and game numbers agree. The final line averages ORR (60%), a Marcel-style projection (30%) and ORR's rates times last season's games (10%).
Season simulation
Each simulated season draws team strength from its uncertainty, sized from the blend's out-of-sample error net of game luck, plus drift during the season. It then plays every game and applies the NHL's standings rules, tiebreakers and playoff bracket.
In-season
After each game day a Kalman-style filter updates the ratings from goals, and from shots when the results carry them, starting from the preseason ratings. The day's games and players are forecast from the updated ratings and known starting goalies, and the rest of the season is simulated again from the current standings.
Evaluation
Every model choice was tuned on earlier seasons and tested walk-forward on later ones. A daily forecast counts for a game only if published before its scheduled puck drop. Season accuracy is evaluated once, after the last regular-season game; figures during the season are provisional.
