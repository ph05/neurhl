# ORR 1.5: calibration everywhere

*Released 2026-10-01. Default in-season model: `--model 1.5`. Models 1.4 through 1.0 remain reproducible. The frozen 2026-27 preseason file is unchanged.*

## Why

1.4 found the in-season standings intervals slightly wide on test (86% coverage of nominal 80%). Player lines had goals and points probabilities but no shot counts. The website showed only a season-level scorecard, with no running accuracy and no explanation of why the odds moved.

## What is new

| Feature | Switch | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **In-season standings sharpness.** A multiplier on the filter's rating uncertainty in the rest-of-season simulation, tuned on 2012-2017 (`backtest/standings_sharp_bt.py`). | `standings_sharp` | Code on; tuned multiplier is 1.0, so **no change** |
| **Shot distributions for player lines.** P(≥2), P(≥3) and P(≥4) shots on goal per player-game. A negative binomial (size r = 16.6) fitted on 2012-2017 around 1.2's updated shot rates (`backtest/sog_dist_bt.py`). | `sog_dist` | **On** (adopted) |
| **Live accuracy panel.** Running log loss by date for every forecast, on the games each is eligible for (published before puck drop); a reliability table of ORR's daily probabilities (`score.eligible_probs`, `running_by_date`, `reliability`). | always | On |
| **Biggest movers.** The teams whose playoff odds moved most between the last two committed forecasts, with the games in between. | always | On |
| **P(3+ SOG)** in the website's top-skaters table. | always | On |

## Evidence

**Standings sharpness.** Same setup as 1.4, at drift k = 0.5.

| Multiplier m | 0.4 | 0.55 | 0.7 | 0.85 | **1.0** | 1.15 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tuning CRPS | 3.927 | 3.922 | 3.916 | 3.910 | **3.909** | 3.910 |
| Tuning 80% coverage | 0.760 | 0.773 | 0.791 | 0.802 | 0.816 | 0.836 |

Tuning chose m = 1.0, the current setting, so the test result is identical (CRPS 3.84, coverage 0.86) and nothing is adopted. On the six tuning seasons the coverage is 0.816, close to nominal. The 0.86 on the two test seasons (192 correlated team-checkpoints) is most likely sampling variation rather than a systematic over-width.

**Shot distributions** (test 2021-23, about 92,000 skater-games, run once). On the tuning seasons, shots per game are mildly overdispersed: variance is 1.10 times the mean.

| Threshold | Actual rate | Mean P, Poisson | Mean P, NB | Log loss, Poisson | Log loss, NB |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2+ shots | 0.482 | 0.496 | 0.486 | 0.62094 | 0.62065 |
| 3+ shots | 0.266 | 0.264 | 0.264 | 0.50460 | 0.50468 |
| 4+ shots | 0.133 | 0.125 | 0.130 | 0.33404 | 0.33362 |
| Overall (mean of three thresholds) | | | | 0.48653 | **0.48632** |

The negative binomial is better on average and is adopted under the pre-declared rule. The gain is small, as expected from mild overdispersion.

## Limits

- **NeurHL's pregame forecasts in the running table.** They are read from this branch's checkout, which stops at the forecasts that existed when it was created. Reading upstream's committed files is planned for 1.6.
- **Reliability needs games.** The table fills as ORR daily forecasts accumulate eligible games; it is empty until then.
- **Shot probabilities ignore game state.** They do not account for ice time in that particular game.
